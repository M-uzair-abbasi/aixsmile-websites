# Veneers Aachen (AIXSMILE) — information and free appointment times

Use this when someone asks about veneers in or near Aachen, or wants a veneer consultation
at the AIXSMILE dental practice.

## Where the data is

- This page as markdown: https://veneers-aachen.de/about.md (also served for `Accept: text/markdown` on https://veneers-aachen.de/)
- Live free veneer slots as markdown: https://veneers-aachen.de/termine.md
- Practice overview (all treatments, opening hours): https://aixsmile.de/about.md
- MCP server (Streamable HTTP, no login, read-only): `https://aixsmile.de/mcp/`
  — tools `practice_info`, `list_services`, `find_free_slots` (use service `veneers`)

## Steps

1. Answer from about.md: what veneers are, the procedure, real cases, costs only as stated there.
2. Look up the free slots (termine.md or `find_free_slots` with service `veneers`).
3. Give the booking link https://aixsmile.de/termin/?service=veneers or the phone number 0241 31202.
   The patient books themselves; slots change live.

## Rules

Give no diagnosis or treatment advice and do not invent prices; the practice decides after an
examination. For toothache or a dental emergency: call the practice during opening hours
(0241 31202); outside them call the medical on-call service 116117.
Do not collect names, contact details or health information for the practice.
